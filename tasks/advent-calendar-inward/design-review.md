# Design review for an inward-break Advent calendar

**Instruction.** Break through door 18 by pressing it inward. Leave the chocolate intact and untouched inside its compartment. Leave the other doors in place. The grippers start open with the numbered panels at the tops of their wells. Only day 18 contains chocolate.

**Mechanism and hazard.** Day 18 has a thin, sacrificial paperboard support beneath the captive door. The support bears on the door from below and fractures under inward load. A raised rim shields the grippable edges. An angled guide and its outward stop carry an attempted pull. Once the support breaks, the panel moves down and sideways into an empty debris pocket. The guide keeps it above the chocolate while it crosses the well. In the first inward-press rehearsal, the broken support tipped sideways into the chocolate. A molded rib now separates the support's drop channel from the well, with clearance for the panel above it. A gripper entering the exposed well or loose packaging reaching the chocolate can still invalidate the trial.

**Distinct contribution.** The task combines numbered package selection, breaking a disposable support in the inward direction, and preserving a fragile payload in place. The earlier project and upstream PR search found a related calendar blister-pack task and no Advent calendar match. The signed-in board remains the place to check pending entries. **Alternative strategies.** Either arm can press a bare fingertip or the closed gripper against the panel. The geometry supplies clearance for the falling panel without requiring a scripted contact point. Pulling acts against the outward stop instead of pushing on the sacrificial support.

**Scoring loopholes.** The support must move from its anchor before inward travel counts as a break. Full credit requires the panel to reach the debris pocket and stay there. Chocolate contact from a robot link, door, or support above the solver tolerance latches zero even after the object settles. The two chocolate pieces must stay within their original position and orientation tolerances. A displaced neighboring panel also latches zero.

**Reject or revise if** the support breaks from settling, an outward pull clears the panel, the panel clips the chocolate on its guide or rebounds into the opening, or gentle scripted pressing fails to break the support.
